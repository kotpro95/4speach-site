Privacy policy

Updated: 6 October 2026

This document explains what data 4Speach collects, why it is needed and what you can do about it. It is written in plain language: if anything is unclear, write to us and we will explain.

Who processes the data

The operator is the 4Speach team. For any question about data processing write to hello@4speach.com or message @speach4 on Telegram.

What we collect

What you enter in the request form yourself:

  • name
  • country
  • phone, Telegram or email — at least one of the three

Technical data collected automatically when you visit the site:

  • IP address, device and browser type, system language
  • which pages you opened and which site you came from
  • on-page activity, through Yandex.Metrica with session recording

Technical data is collected only after you press "Accept" in the cookie banner. If you choose "Essential only", analytics does not start at all.

Separately about the program itself: 4Speach is a cloud product. During a call the audio is sent to our cloud for processing — translation is not technically possible otherwise. See "What happens to the audio of your call".

Why we use it

  • to contact you about your request and onboard you to the beta
  • to help you install and set up the program
  • to understand which parts of the site are useful and improve the product
  • to answer your messages

We do not sell contacts, do not pass them to advertising networks and do not send mail you have not asked for.

Legal basis

The basis is your consent. You give it deliberately: by filling in and submitting the form, and by pressing "Accept" in the cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us.

Who we share data with

For the product to work, some data is processed by our contractors:

  • FormSubmit — forwards form requests to our email
  • Yandex — Yandex.Metrica, website analytics
  • cloud speech recognition, translation and synthesis providers — processing of audio while the program runs

Each of them processes data under its own rules and security terms. We do not pass data to anyone who is not needed for the product to work, and we do not disclose it to third parties on our own initiative.

What happens to the audio of your call

The audio travels over an encrypted channel to the cloud, is recognised, translated and voiced — and comes back to you. We do not publish the recording of your conversation and do not hand it to third parties.

One thing to know in advance: in many countries recording or processing a conversation without the other person knowing is restricted by law. Informing the other person where your local law requires it is your responsibility. On our side, outgoing translation is switched on with a separate button — it never appears in a conversation by itself.

How long we keep it

We keep requests while you remain a user of the product, or until you ask us to delete them. Analytics data is stored in Yandex.Metrica under that service’s rules.

If you ask us to delete your data, we do so within 30 days and confirm it by email.

Your rights

At any time you may:

  • find out what data of yours we hold
  • correct inaccurate data
  • ask us to delete your data
  • withdraw your consent to processing
  • opt out of analytics — clear the site data in your browser and the banner will appear again, where you can choose "Essential only"

Write to hello@4speach.com. We reply within 30 days.

Cookies

The site uses cookies and localStorage to remember your language choice and your answer in the cookie banner — without this the site cannot behave predictably. Yandex.Metrica analytics cookies are set only with your consent.

Children

The product is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you find that a child’s data has reached us, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

The product is in beta and keeps changing. If this policy changes too, we will update the date at the top of the page. Material changes affecting requests already submitted will be sent to the contact you left.
