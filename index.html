What it is

A multi-translator for Zoom, Meet and any other call

4Speach takes the sound from any calling application and instantly translates it into your native language — as voice and as text. And the other way round: it takes your voice from the microphone, translates it and feeds it into your counterpart's speaker in their language. No plugin, no bot in the meeting, no organiser's permission. Your counterpart hears your real voice: timbre, intonation and personal quirks are preserved. The product works fast, and the other person is left with the full impression that you know their language well.

Questions

Frequently asked questions about the product

Will my counterpart know that I use a translator?

Only if you switch outgoing translation on — then they hear a synthesised voice. Incoming translation plays in your headphones and is invisible to them. Many people use only that: they follow the speech and answer themselves.

Where does my conversation go?

The audio travels over an encrypted channel to our cloud on Google infrastructure and is processed there under full security terms. The recording of your conversation is not published and is not passed on to third parties.

Do I need a powerful computer for real-time speech translation?

No. All the heavy lifting happens in the cloud, so no graphics card is needed. An ordinary work laptop with a stable internet connection is enough.

What if the translation gets something important wrong?

That is why the window shows subtitles: original in grey, translation in bold. A doubtful phrase is visible at once and can be checked. Machine translation helps in conversation; it does not replace a lawyer when you sign a contract.

What is the latency of the simultaneous translation?

Up to 1 second. Measured: your speech reaches your counterpart translated in 0.98 s — counted from the end of the sentence to the first sound of the translation. That is the pace of a normal dialogue, not a pause everyone waits through.

Does the speech translator work on Mac?

Right now, Windows 10 and 11. The macOS version is in progress: leave your email below and we will write on release day.

What if it does not suit me?

Try the product on a real call of your own before you pay for anything. If it does not suit you, we refund the money on request within 30 days, no questions asked.
